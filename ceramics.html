The practice

Craft meets architecture

Fishy Ceramics is the tile and ceramics practice of Isabel Kennedy — currently developing a series of tiles inspired by Mumbai. The work sits at the intersection of craft, spirituality, and luxury design.

Each tile series is built around the energy of a person. Star signs, numerology, tarot, and the objects that hold meaning for you are woven into carved tile designs — then silicon-moulded so the master can be reproduced at whatever scale your space calls for.

Think a powder room tiled entirely in your own geometry. A wall hanging that holds your energy. A repeating pattern that is, quietly and completely, yours.

How a commission works

The process begins with sensing your energy

There is no standard brief here. A tile commission with Fishy Ceramics begins with a meeting — in person or online — where Isabel gets to know you. Your astrological chart. Your numerology. The tarot archetypes that resonate. The objects, colours, and symbols that carry meaning in your life.

From that conversation, the tile is designed. A master tile is carved by hand — a unique piece that holds the essence of what emerged. Once the master is made, you choose the scale of your project.

A single framed tile. A small series. A full powder room installation. The master is silicon-moulded and reproduced at whatever quantity your space calls for — from an intimate wall hanging to a complete architectural surface.

→ Powder rooms — a complete bespoke tile scheme for one of the most personal rooms in a home
→ Wall installations — a tile or tile series as an art object, framed or displayed
→ Feature walls — repeating pattern tiles for kitchens, bathrooms, hotel lobbies
→ Gifts — a single tile made for someone, carrying their energy

Corporate ceramics workshops

Alongside individual tile commissions, Isabel runs corporate ceramics workshops — using clay and tile-making as a medium for leadership development, team connection, and creative intelligence.

These aren't art classes. They're leadership experiences that happen to use clay — drawing on Isabel's background as both a ceramicist and executive coach. Teams leave with a different understanding of themselves and each other.
