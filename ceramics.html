The practice
"I look at a flower and become completely absorbed — how many petals, how many fronds. From that fixation, I put it into clay. The piece represents true presence and nature frozen in time."

Flowers

Flowers are a cornerstone of presence — something that promises life, energy, and beauty while reminding us of impermanence. Bottle brush, rose, chamomile — each hand-sculpted, each a moment held in clay.

Fishy — and why that name

As a child, hours were spent in rockpools — almost escaping into the world below. The mystery of the ocean. The name Fishy is the name of the whole practice — and the ocean series is where it began.

Bespoke tile commission · 2025

Bespoke tiles · One person at a time

A tile made for you.

Not a series. Not a collection. A single commission begins with getting to know one person — their star sign, numerology, the tarot archetypes that resonate, the objects and symbols that carry meaning.

From that, a master tile is hand-carved. Once it exists, you decide the scale — one framed tile, a set, a full room. The master is silicon-moulded and reproduced at whatever the space calls for.

A powder room in your own geometry. A wall hanging that holds your energy. A gift that carries something of who someone actually is.

i.

The conversation

We meet — in person or online. Star signs, numerology, personal objects. No brief. Just getting to know you.

ii.

The design

A tile design develops from what emerged. You see it before anything is made.

iii.

The master

One master tile is hand-carved. Your energy, in clay.

iv.

Your scale

One tile, a small series, or a full installation. The master is moulded and reproduced accordingly.

Clay as wellness

A making session. Not an art class.

Clay slows you down. It demands your hands. It cannot be thought through — you have to feel it. In that space between intention and material, something settles.

Sessions are built around pinch pots and freeform making — reacting to what arises, following what the hands want to do. The aim is to feel lighter and more present by the time you leave.

Available for individuals, small groups, and teams. No experience needed. No outcome expected. Past clients include Soho House and Scripe.io.
