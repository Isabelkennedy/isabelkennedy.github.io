The philosophy

The right brain isn't a luxury. It's how you survive.

After 10+ years placing executives across global pharmaceutical and biotech companies — over 10,000 hours of executive interviews, €14M in revenue — I noticed a pattern.

The most sustainably effective leaders weren't just analytically brilliant. They had a creative practice. A physical discipline. A way of staying connected to themselves underneath the pressure.

The ones who didn't? They burned out. Or they succeeded in ways that slowly hollowed them out. Most people ignore the signals until they crash. Then it's 6–12 months of forced recovery — the career cost and opportunity cost of which is immense.

We've been optimised into left-brain-only existence. The antidote is reawakening the right brain — through creativity, movement, and the body's own intelligence — before you're forced to stop. This isn't a hunch. The science is clear.

A framework a decade in the making

The Kennedy Wiggle

Are you in an upward or downward spiral? Good feelings breed more good feelings. Bad feelings breed more bad feelings. The Kennedy Wiggle is how you work with that — not against it. It runs through everything I do.

01

Acknowledge

Where are you right now — upward spiral or downward? Just notice. No judgement.

02

Bring it closer

Sit with it. Let it inform you. Don't push it away. Your body already knows.

03

Do something with it

Move your body. Make something. Create. This is where purpose lives.

Backed by research from Brackett / Yale Emotional Intelligence Van der Kolk / The Body Keeps the Score Loehr & Schwartz / HBR

The Burnout Prevention Programme

This isn't for people who are already on the floor. It's for people who can feel it coming — the Sunday dread, the Thursday pit-of-stomach feeling, the sense that something needs to change before it changes you.

Most people ignore the signals until they crash. Then it's 6–12 months of forced recovery. The career cost and the opportunity cost of that is immense.

"The goal is to choose your path — not to be forced into it by a breakdown."

Over nine weeks, we use the Kennedy Wiggle, colour mapping, body-based decision-making, and daily creative practice to build a different relationship with yourself and your work. Purpose isn't a grand epiphany — it's a quiet signal your body sends, once you know how to listen.

We also work with the Body Compass — a practice for learning to read your own yes and no when analytical thinking alone won't get you there. Used by scientists, senior leaders, entrepreneurs, and creatives who are done running on empty.

This is not therapy. Not psychology. Not for people who don't trust their intuition or won't trust a process. It's for leaders who are ready to use a different kind of intelligence.

Landing

Completion
