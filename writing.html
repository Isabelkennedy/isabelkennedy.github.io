#20daysof

Presence · Noticing · Persistence · Performance

What happens when you show up for twenty days in a row — not to achieve something, but to notice it. #20daysof is a book about the smallest unit of change and what it reveals about how we actually live.

It began as a practice. It became a framework. It is now becoming a book.
